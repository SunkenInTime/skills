# Review criteria

Apply only the requested stage. Record failures and repair them before delivery; inspect changed boundaries again after the repair. Keep evidence in the project's existing notes rather than creating a separate reporting system. The review should not alter other approved sections or trigger unsolicited exports.

## Speech and pacing

- Each kept phrase comes from an intentional take. Cross-take reconstruction has an editorial reason. Inspect source context around ambiguous starts so remnants of a failed take do not precede the clean phrase.
- Review every changed join in sequence, including outgoing and incoming speech. Verify complete final syllables/consonants and complete incoming words. Inspect at least half a second of source beyond proposed endings, as described in pacing.md.
- After all trims and frame rounding, inspect every short clip for an intentionally retained word, sound, or phrase. Repair unexplained one- or two-frame fragments. Intentionally shortened vowel pieces or comedy beats must have a reason, not be rejected merely for being short.
- Review the first five seconds continuously and then the entire edited sequence. Check whether each thought arrives promptly; inspect stretched delivery as well as pauses. Compare the weakest section with calibration references rather than averaging its pacing with better sections.
- Preserve purposeful laughter and comedy timing. Preserve spoken meaning, counts, and names unless the requested edit warrants an understood editorial change.

Evidence: selected take/range plan, repaired join locations, reasons for intentional pauses/short fragments, and actual review method. ASR agreement, silence measurements, and no timeline gaps are supporting evidence only. If listening is unavailable, complete the source/word/waveform checks and describe the result as a provisional review, not an auditioned final.

## Images, framing, and motion

- Every placement supports an identifiable spoken beat. Review asset-free stretches longer than about four seconds and either fill a genuine explanatory gap or retain an intentional face-only beat with a reason.
- Inspect the actual composite at each asset's entrance, representative middle, and exit; include the closest face framing or largest asset size during motion. All bounds remain in the chosen usable region, clear of the face and reserved platform controls. Check headroom and the closing lean-in when moving the face.
- At phone size, the important logo, price, quote, or chart annotation is readable. Text that exists but cannot be read requires a better excerpt or asset.
- Inspect the opening move at start, midpoint, finish, and the next cut. Verify the face stays centered and supporting graphics stay in their own region.
- Check adjacent placements for repetitive images or transitions, overlapping imagery, stale source media, and visible backing that serves no purpose. Preserve the user's deliberate removals.

Evidence: spoken-beat plan, composite frames or directly inspected viewer positions, and any changed crop/bounds. Source thumbnails alone do not show Fusion output. Use native viewer checks when review is in Resolve; use rendered samples when an export is requested.

## Sound

Review each effect with its overlapping speech and following phrase. Preserve working effects, make intended transients audible, and keep speech intelligible through the tail. Verify smooth useful fade-outs, final headroom, and synchronization. Do not change the mix solely because a generic target differs from the measured level. For an images-only pass, retain all audio; where available, compare pre/post audio data or rendered audio hashes to verify this.

## Resolve and handoff

Verify timeline rate and playback rate separately, along with picture/audio ranges, source media availability, and actual native-composition output. Follow resolve.md when an API readback disagrees with visible behavior. Prefer the smallest supported correction; preserve the preceding working version.

Save and leave the edited timeline active at the beginning. Default handoff is inside Resolve, with a short summary and any review limitation. For an explicitly requested export or transfer, complete the file verification and destination-specific confirmation in resolve.md. Verify every affected split clip, not only one representative composition. State user approval only when the user gave it.
