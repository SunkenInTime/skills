# Calibration before editing

Use the media below to recover the shared judgment from the original editing conversation. These small excerpts are bundled with the skill so a fresh task does not need to discover old exports. Their [manifest](../assets/calibration/manifest.json) records sources and status. They are reference excerpts, not media to insert into another video.

## Speech pass

Compare [loose opening](../assets/calibration/loose-opening.mp4) with [tighter opening](../assets/calibration/tighter-opening.mp4). Inspect the phrase handoff in the first seconds: Dara rejected the first transition's waiting time. Match the continuity of the tighter revision while preserving whole words; copying its measured silence alone does not reproduce the effect.

Inspect [midsection revision](../assets/calibration/midsection-revision.mp4) for the shorter connector into "Hermes agent" and the treatment of "chance". This illustrates editing inside hesitant delivery. It is not an endorsed duration target: Dara later still called the entire version slow.

When deciding whether speech rate contributes to drag, compare [1.2x](../assets/calibration/speed-1.2x.mp4) and [1.3x](../assets/calibration/speed-1.3x.mp4). They cover approximately the same opening content at different rates. Neither speed was explicitly selected. Use them to judge intelligibility and energy, not to impose a universal rate.

Before cutting the new source, identify its clean opening take, first phrase handoff, likely slow connector or held syllable, and any intentional pause. If a complete take already works, tighten that take rather than unnecessarily assembling a new intro. Before delivery, compare the new opening and weakest middle passage against the references again.

## Corrective comparisons to carry forward

| Tempting shortcut | Correction established by feedback |
| --- | --- |
| Close all empty timeline gaps | Inspect silence still inside clip heads and tails, then phrase delivery itself. |
| Trust a transcript's word end | Check the source beyond it; "2026" and "twenty" lost audible endings in later edits. |
| Cut every quiet region | Preserve laughter, intentional comedy, and quiet consonants. |
| Fill every three seconds with an asset | Use a relevant image or an intentional face-only beat; omit repetition. |
| Fit a whole screenshot into the box | Make its useful content readable through a relevant crop or a sequence of excerpts. |
| Remove every background | Keep logo cards and restore contrast where transparent graphics disappear. |
| Set effects low enough to avoid clipping | Establish dialogue, then make effects perceptually audible with useful curved tails. |
| Believe a successful API write | Inspect the composed result; missing Fusion layers were caught only in render review. |

## Calibration record

In the project's existing edit notes, record: examples inspected, the new source's weak beat, the intended treatment, and any unavailable review capability. Two or three specific sentences are enough. A filename list without observations does not establish calibration. If bundled media is unavailable, locate the source in the manifest; if neither is accessible, use the documented contrasts and disclose the narrower basis for the review.
